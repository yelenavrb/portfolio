# About me

<div class="profile-section">
<img src="/images/workspace.jpeg" alt="A laptop and keyboard in Yelena's workspace by a sunny window" class="profile-workspace" />

Hi, I’m Yelena – a *Senior Knowledge Manager*.

My goal is simple: make life easier for users and support. When the same question keeps coming up, *I look into why*:

- Maybe the answer is hard to find
- Maybe an internal process is unclear
- Or maybe the product makes you do extra work

Sometimes the solution is a better help article. Sometimes – a clearer internal process. And sometimes it's a *product change*, such as letting users send their logs through the UI.
</div>

I also review designs for new features before they reach users. I check the words, but I don’t stop there. I look at the whole flow and *ask simple questions*: 

- Does this step make sense? 
- Does the user know what to do next? 
- Can we remove the problem instead of writing more text to explain it?

I handle documentation from start to finish, including structure, design, writing, style guides, publishing, localization, analytics, and quality checks. 

This work has given me unusually strong feelings about button text, old screenshots, and error messages that only say *“Something went wrong”*.
