# Localization

## Contribution

- Managed product localization in five languages.
- Manage Crowdin inventories containing 5,500 visible app strings and 3,125 visible website strings.
- Localized app UI, Help Center content, release notes, website content, system emails, and marketing content into four additional languages.
- Localized 2,146+ Russian app strings from January through September 2026.
- Translated and proofread 60+ blog articles.
- Maintain glossaries, translation memories, terminology rules, AI prompts, and localization QA checks.
- Reduced the main application translation memory from approximately 8,000 to 3,000 segments and reviewed 300 glossary terms.
- Standardized Crowdin configuration across four projects.

## Impact

- Kept content available in multiple languages.
- Maintained consistent terminology and tone across products and communications.
- Supported product awareness and engagement across regions.
