# Help Center

## Contribution

- Manage a Help Center with 1,000+ localized article versions across English, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese, and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Created or updated 379+ English documentation articles from January through September 2026.
- Create technical documentation for APIs, CLI tools, browser fingerprinting, cloud phones, proxies, automation, and product features.
- Developed style guides and article templates to keep content consistent.
- Improved SEO, categorization, and structure.
- Worked with Product, Engineering, and Support teams to keep information accurate and relevant.
- Ran content audits and improved articles using user behavior and team feedback.

## Impact

- Maintained 100% documentation delivery SLA compliance from January through September 2026.
- Consolidated two Helpjuice accounts into one, reducing annual subscription costs by approximately $2,400.
- Maintained a reliable source of product knowledge for users and internal teams.
